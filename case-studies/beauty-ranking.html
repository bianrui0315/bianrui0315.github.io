Mirror Light Beauty Ranking

Rui built a bilingual beauty product research and recommendation platform that turns product records, ingredients, regional context, and user preferences into explainable discovery experiences without uploading a personal profile to the backend.

The Product Problem

Beauty discovery is fragmented across rankings, ingredient lists, regional reviews, and shopping pages. Popularity-only lists also hide sample size, freshness, and uncertainty. Mirror Light brings those signals into one research surface while keeping recommendations explainable and clearly separated from medical advice.

Rui's Role

Rui led the product design, responsive React implementation, ranking and personalization logic, Worker APIs, D1 schema and FTS5 search, bulk data preparation, scheduled ETL, privacy model, testing, and Cloudflare deployment.

Product Evidence

The interface supports ranked discovery, cross-product comparison, ingredient and market context, detailed product research, bilingual content, and a dedicated mobile experience.

What Rui Built

  • Explainable recommendation layer: overall, new, sensitive-skin, value, and personalized rankings with a documented 0–100 match score rather than a black-box recommendation.
  • Privacy-first personalization: skin profile, goals, budget, texture preferences, and optional weather context stay on the device; exact location is not stored in D1.
  • Searchable product data: D1 tables and FTS5 indexes support bounded product, brand, category, market, and INCI ingredient discovery with paginated detail APIs.
  • Production data workflow: streaming Open Beauty Facts preparation, content hashing, staging tables, source attribution, quality gates, audit records, and a weekly incremental ETL path.
  • Single edge deployment: one Cloudflare Worker serves the React application, same-origin APIs, D1 queries, optional weather lookup, and Cron-driven updates without a separate CORS surface.
  • Consumer-ready workflow: bilingual UI, regional views, random discovery, local wishlist persistence, and PDF/TXT export turn research into a usable decision artifact.

Engineering Decisions

List queries read only bounded summary fields, while complete ingredients load on demand. Controlled sort enums map to fixed SQL fragments, requests use bound parameters, stale ranking requests are canceled, and failed ETL runs preserve the last successful data rather than publishing partial state.

Trust Boundaries

The live experience clearly marks demonstration scores and review-pending catalog records. Rankings are research aids, not medical or purchasing advice, and source licensing, formula versions, and regional availability remain visible verification concerns.

Capability Signal

This project demonstrates Rui's ability to connect consumer product judgment with recommendation logic, privacy-aware UX, data quality engineering, full-stack TypeScript, SQL search, scheduled pipelines, and edge infrastructure.
